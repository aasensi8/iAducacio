What is artificial intelligence?

According to the OECD, an AI system It is a system based on machines that, from the inputs it receives and explicit or implicit objectives, infers how to produce predictions, contents, recommendations or decisions that can influence physical or virtual environments. Systems can have different degrees of autonomy and adaptation after deployment.

Didactic paraphrase in Catalan; it is not a literal citation. Definition revised in 2023 and explained by the OECD in 2024. [1]

The European Regulation of AI It also defines the system for its ability to infer results from inputs, with several levels of autonomy and possible subsequent adaptation. The legal definition is in Article 3.1. [2]

AI is wider than a chat

It can classify images, estimate a demand, recommend content or generate text. The type of result and degree of autonomy depends on the system. [1]
