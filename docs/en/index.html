AI opens up possibilities. You bring the judgment.

A space to explore AI from a teacher’s perspective and put it to work for learning.

OUR STARTING POINT

Pedagogy first.
Technology second.

What can AI make possible that was previously impossible or extremely difficult?

AI belongs in education only when it addresses a real learning need and opens opportunities that were previously impractical.

01Why?

Identify the educational need: which learning challenge are we solving?

02What for?

Demand distinctive value: what does AI enable that we could not previously provide?

03What improves?

Check the impact: do understanding, autonomy or learning improve?

The point is not to do the same things with AI, but to discover what more we can do to improve education.

Read iAducació’s four commitments

Our commitments

  • Purpose before toolsWe do not adopt technology simply because it is new.
  • Learning, not just efficiencySaving time does not necessarily mean learning more.
  • Human thinking and judgmentAI supports learning; it does not replace cognitive effort, human relationships or teacher responsibility.
  • Evidence of impactEvery use should be justified, supervised and evaluated with evidence.
